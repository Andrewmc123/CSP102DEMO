What It Takes to Become a Robotics Engineer

Robotics Engineering is a field that combines technology, programming, mechanics, artificial intelligence, and robotics to create intelligent systems that can help humans solve real-world problems. Students at UAT learn how to build robots, drones, autonomous vehicles, and advanced smart systems using both hardware and software.

Robotics Engineering Degree Objectives

  • Design and complete robotic and embedded systems solutions that address real-world situations and challenges.
  • Demonstrate embedded microprocessor system and circuit skills.
  • Develop mechanical control systems using transducers, actuators, sensors, and robotic platforms.
  • Examine and assess a variety of applications within the field of robotics.
  • Model, analyze, and design systems that integrate hardware and software to control autonomous systems.
  • Implement artificial intelligence and data systems into robotic platforms.

Important Robotics Engineering Courses

Students in Robotics Engineering take courses such as Introduction to Programming, C/C++ Programming, Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Robot Navigation, Embedded Programming, Mechatronics, Digital Vision and Sensor Processing, Autonomous Ground Vehicles, and Autonomous Aerial Vehicles.

These courses help students gain hands-on experience working with modern robotic systems and prepare them for future careers in engineering and technology.
