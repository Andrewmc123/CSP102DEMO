Robotics Engineering Objectives

Robotics Engineering combines programming, mechanics, and artificial intelligence to build smart systems that solve real-world problems.

Degree Objectives

  • Design robotic and embedded systems that solve real-world problems.
  • Demonstrate embedded microprocessor and circuit skills.
  • Develop control systems using sensors, actuators, and robotic platforms.
  • Examine a variety of applications within the field of robotics.
  • Design systems that integrate hardware and software to control robots.
  • Implement artificial intelligence into robotic platforms.
